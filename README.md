## About me

I'm a freshly graduated EE with a focus on radio technology and embedded systems. My thesis was a passive RF detection and positioning prototype — a coherent dual-channel SDR system with angle-of-arrival estimation and a full geolocation pipeline.

I like building things that touch both hardware and software: firmware that actually runs on silicon, RF systems that decode real signals, and tools that solve real problems. When I'm not doing that, I'm doing canicross with my Blue Heeler **Hati** 🐕 — she's the reason half my side projects exist.

Open to opportunities in RF engineering, signal processing, and embedded systems — including relocation to Germany or Switzerland.
